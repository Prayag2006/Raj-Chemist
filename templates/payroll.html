How is this calculated?

Working hours are computed by taking the time difference between an employee's "Check In" and the subsequent "Check Out" on the same day. If an employee only checks in without a check out, those hours may not be counted automatically until a manual adjustment is made. The final salary is the product of the total calculated hours and the employee's configured hourly rate.
